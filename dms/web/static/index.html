    What each part detects

    • Drowsiness — eye-closure (EAR, PERCLOS P80), blink duration, microsleeps ≥ 1 s, yawns ≥ 1.5 s, and a Conv-Transformer over 30 s of features (alert / low-vigilant / drowsy).
    • Distraction — head pose + gaze: eyes off road ≥ 3 s, and repeated glances totalling ≥ 10 s in 30 s (Euro NCAP 2026 definitions). YOLO detects phone use, drinking, smoking.
    • Emotion — facial-expression CNN (7 classes). Anger/fear raise the risk of other hazards and trigger a "stress" suggestion when sustained.
    • Per-driver calibration — the first seconds learn your open-eye shape and neutral head pose, so thresholds adapt to each driver and camera position.

    What we do differently

    1. One system for drowsiness, distraction and emotion, fused into one explainable risk score — every alert says why.
    2. Alert rules follow the Euro NCAP 2026 driver-engagement definitions.
    3. Per-driver calibration used both live and during training.
    4. Tiny temporal model (≈174k params, <1 ms on CPU) that needs no stored face images — privacy-friendly.
    5. Honest evaluation: people in the test set are never seen in training; cross-dataset tests; latency reported.

    Product vision: dash cam → phone

    • Driver-facing (IR) dash cam streams over its own Wi-Fi — no internet needed.
    • Phone app (Android foreground service) runs the same models on-device while the screen is locked.
